## Context

Field Notes is a small prototype for capturing the edges of ongoing technical work without forcing every observation into a finished article.

## Constraints

The system should be quick to write to, easy to search later, and explicit about uncertainty. It should not imply that an untested observation is a conclusion.

## Architecture

The current shape is deliberately simple: structured frontmatter for durable metadata, file-based content for portability, and a presentation layer that can evolve independently of the notes themselves.

## Verification

The prototype is verified through content validation and route resolution. Its main limitation is that search quality and long-term retrieval patterns have not yet been evaluated against a larger corpus.
