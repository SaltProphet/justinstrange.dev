## Overview

Quiet Inventory is a concept for documenting the operational edges around a system: owners, assumptions, interfaces, failure modes, and the evidence behind each claim.

## Why it matters

Architecture diagrams often describe what should exist while leaving the social and operational dependencies implicit. This model starts from those dependencies instead.

## Limitations

This is a framing exercise, not a shipped product. The next useful test is a small inventory on a real system with a known change horizon.
