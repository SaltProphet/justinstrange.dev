AI Inferred Intent Drift

An investigation into inferred intent, recurring wording, propagation, and downstream effects across AI-assisted work.

← Research

Starting point

Justin Strange’s account, preserved in the research evidence register:

The focus on this stems from the recent trouble I have been having with inferred intent across ALL AI platforms that I use. Every single one of them. Every single model. No matter what. Constantly contrst framing, meta analysis commentary, adversarial strawman rebuttals, etc. Degrading every aspect of my work. I still don't know how faar back it goes, or one single catalyst. So it requires a full audit of my system files, projects, repos, documents, all of it. Just to find the neagtive wording and phrases. Its resulted in poor information, suggestions, bad planning advice, bad career advice, poor work decisions....

Research questions

  • How far back do the reported patterns appear in the available records?
  • Is there a single catalyst, or are there multiple independent occurrences?
  • Where did unwanted interpretations first enter the available system of work?
  • How did wording propagate across conversations, repositories, documents, project plans, and persistent context?
  • When did an interpretation become treated as a project fact, requirement, or instruction?
  • Did any identified interpretation affect planning, information quality, career advice, work decisions, or agent actions?

Audit method

The working research plan records this sequence:

  • Inventory sources, formats, time coverage, and access limitations.
  • Hash and preserve originals; search copies without changing source files.
  • Find candidate wording and preserve surrounding context, locations, timestamps, and available authorship records.
  • Trace reappearance and wording changes across conversations, files, and Git history.
  • Compare original requests with later plans and actions.
  • Record source evidence, analysis, recommendations, and unknowns in separate fields.

Implementation foundation

The proposed implementation extends the existing agent-data mapping pipeline with occurrence scanning, attribution, statement lineage, and timeline reporting. The current Phase 4 plan is recorded in codex-map.
